Co-Op Frontline Flank Mechanics

In Enshrouded, backstab damage is positional—not weapon-restricted. Whenever your melee co-op partner holds boss or elite aggro, strafe behind the target's shoulder line:

  • Backstab Mastery (+60%) multiplies ranged bow hits from behind.
  • Skill Shot (+60%) stacks when hitting rear weak-points or heads.
  • Shell Shock (2s Stun) locks down packs around your frontline tank while Blessed Arrows refunds the mana on crits.
